LORE
Before BLDS had a name, before the symbol existed, before the squad was formed...
There were only individuals trying to survive their own darkness. Some were ignored. Some were underestimated. Some failed over and over again until they stopped expecting anyone to believe in them.

They watched others rise faster. Watched others gain recognition. Watched people quit the moment things became difficult. And slowly, they realized something:
Most people only shine when the world is bright. But very few can continue moving forward when everything turns dark. That realization became the beginning of BLDS.

Not a squad created for fame. Not a kingdom built on arrogance. Not a place for people searching only for victory. But a dominion built by those who refused to disappear.
THE BLACKLIGHT CORE
At the center of the eclipse lies the Core. A single star that continues to shine no matter how dark the sky becomes. The Core represents the inner purpose carried by every member of BLDS.

Not a purpose given by the world. Not a purpose created by attention or popularity, but a purpose discovered through hardship.

It is the reminder that: even when everything around you changes... even when people leave... even when failure surrounds you... your inner light can still remain alive.

BLDS believes the strongest people are not those who never fall. The strongest are the ones who continue standing after every fall. That is why the Core never disappears. Because purpose cannot be erased by fear.
THE TEN FLAMES OF BLDS (5 Greater & 5 Lesser)
GREATER FLAMES
I. Vision
The ability to see direction even when others are lost.
II. Discipline
To continue growing even when nobody is watching.
III. Loyalty
To stand together through victory and defeat.
IV. Ambition
The desire to surpass limits and continue evolving.
V. Resilience
The strength to rise again after every fall.
LESSER FLAMES
I. Hope
The light that remains during difficult times.
II. Awareness
The ability to stay conscious and understand reality.
III. Patience
Growth takes time and endurance.
IV. Courage
The will to move forward despite fear.
V. Unity
The bond that turns individuals into something greater. Though smaller, these flames are just as important. Because even the brightest Core will fade without what supports it.
Five flames grant strength. Five flames preserve it.
Together, they protect the Core.
